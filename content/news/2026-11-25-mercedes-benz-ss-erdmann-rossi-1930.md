Most of the cars we bring to Assetto Corsa were built by the thousand. This one wasn't. There is one of it.

When we opened the 3D model, we didn't know exactly what we had: a supercharged Mercedes-Benz SS roadster, beautifully
made, with no name on it. The answer was hiding in its textures. Tucked into the sheet that holds the dials and badges
were two little plates: the coachbuilder's, **Erdmann & Rossi, Berlin-Halensee**, and one that said **1930**. That
pointed to a single car, chassis **36348**.

![The SS in racing white with red leather](/images/assetto-corsa/mercedes-benz-ss-erdmann-rossi-1930/shot-front.jpg "Racing white, red leather")

Its story goes like this. Daimler-Benz built 36348 in June 1930 as a two-seat works racer, on the SS chassis with the
big 7.1 litre M 06 six and its Roots supercharger. In 1934 Erdmann & Rossi, one of Berlin's great coachbuilders,
gave it a new body for its owner, Werner Lüps: that long, low sport roadster with the flowing wings, the swept tail
and the spare wheel set into it. Made by hand, for one man. Seventy-one years after it was built it took Best of Show
at Pebble Beach, the most coveted prize in the concours world. Today it's black with white leather, and you'll find that
skin in the box.

![Two headlamps, two spot lamps, two horns and the star](/images/assetto-corsa/mercedes-benz-ss-erdmann-rossi-1930/shot-grille.jpg "The front")

The SS is a brute in a dinner jacket. The six makes 170 PS on its own. Put your foot all the way down and the
blower engages, spinning at three times engine speed, and you get 225 PS and that famous whine, which we built into
the sound so you hear the boost come in. Four speeds, a long 2.48 axle, 190 km/h flat out, nearly two tonnes on
skinny 6.50-20 tyres and mechanical drums. It wants a brave driver and a lot of road.

![Black over red, the spare in the tail](/images/assetto-corsa/mercedes-benz-ss-erdmann-rossi-1930/shot-rear.jpg "From behind")

We put a lot of hours into it. The dials got period faces: the big rev counter in the middle of the engine-turned
dash, the speedometer beside it, and they glow at night. The doors open from the front, because they're hinged at
the back. There's a full chassis underneath, with the axle, leaf springs and propeller shaft moving with the
suspension, plus wipers, rim blur on the wire wheels, Kunos-style damage and dirt (set Damage above 0 % in the
assists), and a driver and crew dressed for each skin. Four skins: black with red leather, black with white leather
like 36348 today, racing white with red leather, and the model's own pale silver with cream.

![In the dark of the showroom](/images/assetto-corsa/mercedes-benz-ss-erdmann-rossi-1930/shot-night.jpg "At night")
