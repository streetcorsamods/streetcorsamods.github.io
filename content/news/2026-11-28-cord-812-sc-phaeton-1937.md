Of all the cars we've built so far, this is the one that asked the most of us. Ten days, more passes than I can
count, a lot of "almost, but not yet". Today it's done, and I'm very happy with how it came out.

![Geneva Blue](/images/assetto-corsa/cord-812-sc-phaeton-1937/geneva-blue.jpg "Geneva Blue")

If you don't know the story, here it goes. November 1935, New York Auto Show. Errett Lobban Cord's company shows a
car designed by a young Gordon Buehrig, and the crowd is so thick that people stand on the bumpers of other cars
to get a look. No radiator grille,
just wrap-round louvres on a long "coffin" nose. No running boards. Front-wheel drive. And the headlamps are not
there until you want them: you turn a crank on the dash and they rise out of the fenders. Nothing on an American road
looked like it. Two years and fewer than three thousand cars later, the company was gone. The design never left.

![Palm Beach Tan](/images/assetto-corsa/cord-812-sc-phaeton-1937/palm-beach-tan.jpg "Palm Beach Tan")

Ours is the 1937 812 SC, the supercharged one: a Lycoming V8 with a Schwitzer-Cummins blower, 170 bhp and that
centrifugal whine climbing with the revs, which we built into its own engine sound. The four-speed box sits ahead of
the engine and you pick the next gear with the little Bendix pre-selector on the column. In the game it's a big,
soft, front-driven boulevard car with more go than you expect from 1937.

![Rich Maroon](/images/assetto-corsa/cord-812-sc-phaeton-1937/rich-maroon.jpg "Rich Maroon")

Honestly, this one was a long road. The 3D model we started from, by annaandrei, is a lovely low-poly game prop, and
it gave us the shape; nearly everything you see up close we rebuilt on top of it, from period photos. The
headlamps alone took eight passes before the lid fitted the fender the way it should, and they really do pop up
when you switch the lights on. The dash was laid out from a head-on photo, millimetre by millimetre. The hood, cowl
and trunk are real separate panels now, not lines drawn on the paint. And a review on OverTake told us our cars
lacked "the CSP bling", so this one got it: working odometers, clock, oil pressure and ammeter, refracting headlamp
glass, and the trunk lid, the cowl vents and the fuel filler door open on Extra A, B and C. Thank you for that one,
it made the car better.

![Ivory with black leather](/images/assetto-corsa/cord-812-sc-phaeton-1937/ivory.jpg "Ivory")

Twelve skins, all in the real 1936-37 Cord colours with their leather: Geneva Blue, Palm Beach Tan, Rich Maroon,
Cigarette Cream, Cool Orchard Green, Ganges Green and the rest. Opening doors, wipers, moving suspension underneath,
damage and dirt, four LODs.
