There's a new car in [AC Mods](/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-starfire-98-1957/): the 1957 Oldsmobile Starfire Ninety-Eight
convertible. In 1957 this was as far up the Oldsmobile catalogue as you could go: $3,838, only 8,278 built, a long, low
convertible with a chrome spear down each flank and that two-tone cove sweeping back to the rear fenders.

![The Starfire 98 in Festival Red with an Alcan White cove](/images/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-starfire-98-1957/festival-red.jpg "Festival Red")

Under the hood is the Rocket T-400: 371 cubic inches, 9.5:1 and a four-barrel, good for 277 bhp and 400 lb-ft. It
drives through the four-speed Jetaway Hydra-Matic, with power steering and power drums as standard, like the real
thing. Every number in the physics comes from Oldsmobile's own 1957 specifications, and the engine sound was made for
it in engine-sim.

The model is OUTPISTON's, from Sketchfab. The outside was lovely; the cabin needed a lot of work. I rebuilt it from
photographs of surviving cars: the chrome band that runs across the whole dash with the oval rocket speedometer set
into it, the heater, gear and air-conditioning strip, the Wonder Bar radio, the clock, the ivory roll, the globe
emblem on the glovebox, the scalloped steering wheel, and the pleated two-tone benches and door cards. With the top
down you'll see all of it, so it had to be right.

![Artesian Blue](/images/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-starfire-98-1957/artesian-blue.jpg "Artesian Blue")

It arrives with everything our cars carry now. The rear axle, leaf springs, prop shaft and dual exhaust move with the
suspension. There's Kunos-style damage and dirt, glass and lamps that crack in a hard hit, wipers that sweep, and on
Custom Shaders Patch real low and high beams, turn signals and backup lamps. You get six onboard cameras, and the
driver and the pit crew dress to match each colour.

![Jade Mist](/images/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-starfire-98-1957/jade-mist.jpg "Jade Mist")

Twelve skins, all 1957 Oldsmobile colours, each with its own colour-keyed leather: Festival Red, Artesian Blue, Alcan
White, Jade Mist, Charcoal, Banff Blue, Rose Mist, Coronado Yellow, Gold Mist, Juneau Gray, Allegheny Green and
Cutlass Bronze. It's driven top down, always: the model has no raised top, and honestly, on a car like this, why
would you want one?

![Coronado Yellow](/images/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-starfire-98-1957/coronado-yellow.jpg "Coronado Yellow")
