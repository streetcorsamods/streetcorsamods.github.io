Version 1.2 of the [Auburn 851 Supercharged Speedster](/assetto-corsa/auburn-851-sc-speedster-1935/) is out, and it
gets everything the Ford pickup got last week, and a bit more.

Under the car, the rear axle now rides with the wheels, the leaf springs flex with it, and the front beam tilts over the
bumps. You won't see it from the seat, but put a replay on, drop the camera low beside a wheel and watch it go over a
kerb.

Hit something and the paint gets scratched, the windscreen and the lamp lenses crack, and the right half of the hood
comes loose and flaps. Run wide onto the grass and the dust builds up. The wire wheels blur at speed, there are six
onboard cameras, and the driver and the pit crew dress to match each of the fourteen skins.

The lights got a proper going-over too. The headlamps reach further on Custom Shaders Patch, the two spot lamps on the
windscreen posts light the road, the stop lamps glow behind and there's a little lamp on the plate now. While checking
them we found that the tail lamps disappeared when the car was more than 45 metres away, which is exactly when the
driver behind needs them at night. Fixed: they stay lit at every distance now, and so do the dials when you watch the
car from outside.

Inside, the door corners and their cords were reworked, the dash ends now run smoothly into the side panels, and the
four exhaust pipes run straight and evenly spaced through the fender.

Download the new zip from the car's page and drop it onto Content Manager over the old one. It still needs Custom Shaders
Patch 0.2.11 or newer for the Dual-Ratio axle. And as always, it's open to anybody who wants to improve it, in its SVN
repository on SourceForge.
