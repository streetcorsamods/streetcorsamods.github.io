Street Corsa **0.1.3** is out. Get it on the [downloads](/downloads/) page; it installs over any earlier version.

![A Chevy 283 on the stand, the right way round](/images/mods/street-rod/engine-stand.jpg "A Chevy 283 on the stand, the right way round")

- **Every engine the right way round.** The twelve engine packs are fixed (see
  [the news](/news/2026-11-11-engines-right-way-round/)), and the pictures on this site are shot again.
- **Street Rod 0.1.1.** The carburettors, air cleaners and blower that fit any engine are the right way round too.
  Every rim and tyre has a model again, and two wheels that faced the wrong way are fixed.
- **Lamps cast shadows indoors.** The garage, the shops, the car lot and the paint booth are lit by their lamps, so
  cars and props stand on the floor, day and night.
- **Smoother moving scenes.** A car driving in, the lift, the engine hoist and a revving engine cost less to draw.
- **Your car comes home after a race.** Whenever it can still run, it drives into the garage. A car that won't run is
  towed home.
- **The garage's wheels and springs.** A tyre you take off is gone from the car, and a bare rim shows and can be picked.

To update a setting or an engine pack, install the new zip from the main screen: **Mod Manager → Install mods**.
Everything that changed is in the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
