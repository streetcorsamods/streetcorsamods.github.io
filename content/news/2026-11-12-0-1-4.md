Street Corsa **0.1.4** is out, with **Street Rod 0.4.0**. Get them on the [downloads](/downloads/) page; the game
installs over any earlier version and your saves carry over.

![The painter at work in Street Rod's paint booth](/images/mods/street-rod-shops/paint-booth.jpg "The painter at work in Street Rod's paint booth")

Street Rod's places are mods of their own now, each with its own version:

- [The garage](/mods/street-rod-garage/): the workshop the game is played from, and everything standing in it.
- [The gas station](/mods/street-rod-gas-station/): Crossroads Gas and its attendant.
- [The shops](/mods/street-rod-shops/): the paint booth, the part and machine shops, and the warehouse.
- [Main Street](/mods/street-rod-main-street/): the street the racers meet on, its diners and the kit it's laid out with.

They all come inside Street Rod's download: to update, install the new Street Rod zip from the main screen,
**Mod Manager → Install mods**, and it puts the setting and its four places in.

The split is for modders: a mod that plays in Street Rod can put in a garage, a station, a room or street pieces of its
own, or take Street Rod's out. Each place's files are on
[SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/), and the [modding guide](/tutorials/modding/guide/) starts at
the beginning. Everything that changed is in the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
