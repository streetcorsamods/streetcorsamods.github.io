Version 1.5 of the [Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme Holiday Sedan](/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-cutlass-supreme-holiday-sedan-1971/)
is out, and it is the biggest update this car has had.

The front doors open now. That sounds like a small thing, but the model's doors were a bare skin, so they got the lot:
painted edges, hinges, latches, lock knobs, a rubber seal along the top and brushed sill plates with the emblem. The
wipers work too. On the real car they hide under the edge of the hood, so ours park at the base of the windscreen.

The lights took the longest. A 1971 Cutlass has four sealed beams, and now they work like the real ones on Custom
Shaders Patch: low beam on the outer pair, and the inner pair joins in when you go to high beam. They come up slowly,
the way a filament does, and after a few evenings of driving they are neither blinding nor too dim. There are turn
signals (amber in the parking lamps, red in the tail lamps), backup lamps, side markers, little lamps over the rear
plate, and the dome lamp comes on when you open a door. Thanks to voraciousmyth, who told us the old headlights were
too dim.

Under the car there is a rear axle and a prop shaft that follow the wheels (the model had neither). Hit something and
the paint scratches and the glass and lamps crack; run through the grass and the dust builds up. The Super Stock wheels
blur at speed, there are six onboard cameras, and the driver and the pit crew dress to match each of the sixteen skins.
