Street Corsa **0.1.7** is out, with **Street Rod 0.5.1**. Get both on the [downloads](/downloads/) page: the game
installs over any earlier version, and installing Street Rod from **Mod Manager → Install mods** brings its places
with it.

![Gardena Machine & Engine](/images/mods/street-rod-shops/machine-shop.jpg "Gardena Machine & Engine")

**The machine shop is a real engine shop now.** Walk into Gardena Machine & Engine and the blocks go on a column
boring bar, the cranks into a long crank grinder with its wheel in the guard, under the sign. Round them stand a
hydraulic press, lathes, a head surfacer, bench and floor drill presses, an engine crane with a customer's 454
hanging from it, a big-block on a stand, and an overhead crane running down the side of the shop.

![The shop floor](/images/mods/street-rod-shops/machine-shop-floor.jpg "The shop floor")

And the clutter of a shop that works for a living: a belt-drive compressor, welding bottles on their hand truck by the
hot tank, a red rollaway, a vise and pistons on the workbench, a valve grinder on the head bench, oil cans and drums,
caged lamps over the machines and a 1950s clock on the wall.

For modders, the [Street Rod shops](/mods/street-rod-shops/) 1.1.0 are on SourceForge. The machines and props are
models from Sketchfab (CC BY 4.0) and Poly Haven (CC0), their makers named on the mod's page; the boring bar and the
crank grinder are our own.

Everything that changed is in the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
