There's a new member of the Street Corsa fleet, and it's a working truck: the 1950 Chevrolet 3100, the half-ton
Advance-Design pickup. If you grew up anywhere near a farm, a hardware store or a small town, you've probably seen one
of these, rounded fenders, five-bar grille and a bed full of whatever needed carrying.

![The 3100 in Swift Red](/images/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/shot-front.jpg "Swift Red")

This one took a while. The model came as CAD, not as a game mesh: Oner Yildirim's beautiful "1950 Chevy" from GrabCAD.
Turning engineering surfaces into something Assetto Corsa can draw was a project of its own, and then the real work
started. We had photographs of a restored Swift Red truck, and we kept going back to them: the hood emblems were traced
letter by letter, the fenders hollowed out and undercoated, the grille given its painted inner bars and a radiator
behind them, and the stake racks put where they really go, standing in the bed's stake pockets.

![The CHEVROLET 3100 emblem on the hood side](/images/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/shot-emblem.jpg "Hood emblem")

The cab got the most love. Open the door (yes, the doors open, on their concealed hinges) and you'll find the
body-colour dash with a working speedometer and combination gauge, the speaker grille, the heater down by your feet,
a dome lamp and padded visors, stamped door panels and a plain padded bench. It's a truck: it should feel like one
when you sit in it.

![Door open: the bench, the door panel and the dash](/images/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/shot-door.jpg "Door open")

Under it sits the 216 Thriftmaster six, 92 bhp and 176 lb-ft, through a three-speed on the column. The physics come
from Chevrolet's own 1950 truck sheets, the torque curve included, and the engine sound is our engine-sim Chevy six.
It is not fast. It is not supposed to be. It's a lovely thing to drive slowly down a country road with the window
down.

![From above](/images/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/shot-top.jpg "From above")

![The tailgate](/images/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/shot-rear.jpg "Tailgate")

It comes with everything our cars carry now: the axle, springs and front beam move with the suspension, Kunos-style
damage and dirt (set Damage above 0 % in the assists), glass and lamps that crack, a tailgate and bumpers that come
loose after a hard hit, wipers, six onboard cameras, and a driver and pit crew dressed to match. Twelve skins, all
1950 Chevrolet truck colours, from Swift Red and Forester Green to Omaha Orange and Armour Yellow.

![Lamps lit in the hangar](/images/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/shot-night.jpg "Lamps lit")

Download it from its page in [AC Mods](/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/) and drop the zip onto Content
Manager. Like all our cars, it's open to anybody who wants to make it better: its files are in an
[SVN repository on SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/chevrolet-3100-pickup-1950/). Our work is
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The model is Oner Yildirim's: we've asked him for permission to share it, and we'll take the truck
down if he says no. Thank you, Oner, for such a beautiful model.

Load something in the bed and take it for a drive.
