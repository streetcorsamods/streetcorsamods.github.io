All eight cars on [AC Mods](/assetto-corsa/) have a new version, and they now wear our own licence plates.

![The Street Corsa rear plate and the dealer plate](/images/assetto-corsa/plates/house-plates.jpg "The Street Corsa rear plate and the dealer plate")

At the back there's a black plate with stamped yellow characters, a raised border and rim, and the chrome Street Corsa
script where a state name would go. Each car has its own registration: 32 FRD on the Ford pickup, 60 IMP on the
Impala, 71 SAT on the Satellite. Where the car carries a plate up front, it gets the kind of advertising plate
dealers bolted on in the fifties and sixties: the script and our address. The plates come with normal maps, so the
stamped edges catch the light as the car moves, not just in the paint.

The new versions:

- [Ford V8 Pickup 1932](/assetto-corsa/ford-v8-pickup-1932/) 1.3: rear plate on the tailgate, the dealer plate on the headlamp bar
- [Chevrolet Master DeLuxe Coupe 1937](/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-master-deluxe-1937/) 1.1: rear plate on the trunk lid
- [Buick Roadmaster Skylark 1953](/assetto-corsa/buick-skylark-1953/) 1.2: rear plate in the bumper
- [Buick Roadmaster 1957](/assetto-corsa/buick-roadmaster-1957/) 1.1: front and rear
- [Chevrolet Impala Sedan 1960](/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-impala-sedan-1960/) 1.4: front and rear, on the ribbed bumper panels
- [Buick Riviera 1963](/assetto-corsa/buick-riviera-1963/) 1.2: front and rear, in the plate frames
- [Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme Holiday Sedan 1971](/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-cutlass-supreme-holiday-sedan-1971/) 1.1: front and rear
- [Plymouth Satellite Sebring 1971](/assetto-corsa/plymouth-satellite-sebring-1971/) 1.1: front and rear

The skin pictures on each car's page are new too, rendered in Content Manager with the plates on. Download the new
zip from the car's page and drop it onto Content Manager over the old one. Every car is still open to anybody who
wants to improve it, in its SVN repository on SourceForge.
