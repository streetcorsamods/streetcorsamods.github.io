We've released six cars for **Assetto Corsa itself**. They don't need Street Corsa: drag the zip onto Content Manager
and drive. They have a page of their own on the site, [AC Mods](/assetto-corsa/), apart from the game's downloads.

![The 1963 Buick Riviera in Marlin Blue](/images/assetto-corsa/buick-riviera-1963/marlin-blue.jpg "The 1963 Buick Riviera in Marlin Blue")

- [Ford V8 Pickup 1932](/assetto-corsa/ford-v8-pickup-1932/): the closed-cab pickup with the 221 flathead V8, wire
  wheels and an ahooga horn.
- [Chevrolet Master DeLuxe Coupe 1937](/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-master-deluxe-1937/): the business coupe with the Blue
  Flame six and Knee-Action front end.
- [Buick Roadmaster Skylark 1953](/assetto-corsa/buick-skylark-1953/): the 50th-anniversary convertible, 1,690 built,
  on Kelsey-Hayes wire wheels.
- [Buick Roadmaster 1957](/assetto-corsa/buick-roadmaster-1957/): the Riviera hardtop with the 364 Nailhead and the
  horizontal speedometer.
- [Buick Riviera 1963](/assetto-corsa/buick-riviera-1963/): the first-year Riviera, 401 Nailhead and walnut console.
- [Plymouth Satellite Sebring 1971](/assetto-corsa/plymouth-satellite-sebring-1971/): the hardtop with the 383
  four-barrel and TorqueFlite.

Each one has its physics written from the factory figures, an engine sound made in engine-sim from its own engine,
working gauges, and the colours it came in, named as the maker named them: 71 skins between the six. Custom Shaders
Patch is recommended.

They're open to anybody who wants to make them better: each car's files are in an SVN repository on
[SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/), and the downloads are there too. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
