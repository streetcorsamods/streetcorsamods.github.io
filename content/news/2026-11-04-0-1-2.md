Street Corsa **0.1.2** is out. Get it on the [downloads](/downloads/) page; it installs over 0.1.1 or 0.1.0.

- **A new racer window at the diner.** The race, the matchup and the stakes side by side, and the track picked from
  cards with pictures.
- **A rival's reputation tells you more:** what their standing means and how they stack up against you.
- **The race result card in Assetto Corsa says what was at stake**, pink slips included, and is easier to read.
- **New Assetto Corsa cars show up in your next game** without restarting Street Corsa.
- Settings in two columns, one Save button, and the credits on a screen of their own.

The settings and engine packs are the same as before. Everything that changed is in the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
