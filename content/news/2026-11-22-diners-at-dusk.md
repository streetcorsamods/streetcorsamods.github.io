[Street Corsa 0.1.6](/news/2026-11-22-0-1-6/) gave each of Street Rod's four diners a place of its own in the new
town. Here's what that looks like from behind the wheel, on a Monday evening in June 1970.

![The diners on the map](/images/mods/street-rod/diners-map.jpg "Pick a diner and drive down to see who's in")

It starts at the map. Each diner shows how many racers are in tonight, and whether they run in your class yet: the
regulars keep to their own. Pick one and you drive there yourself, through town.

![Pulling up at the Main Street Diner](/images/mods/street-rod/diners-main-street.jpg "Pulling up at the Main Street Diner")

The Main Street Diner is downtown, among the shops and the palms. Pull into the lot and you can go into the diner,
cruise the streets or head for another one.

![Downtown at dusk](/images/mods/street-rod/diners-downtown.jpg "Downtown at dusk")

![A side street in town](/images/mods/street-rod/diners-traffic.jpg "A side street in town")

The drive across is part of it. There's traffic on the way, cabs and wagons on the move and cars parked along
the curbs, with the mountains behind the rooftops.

![On the strip by the Starlite](/images/mods/street-rod/diners-strip.jpg "On the strip by the Starlite")

![The Starlite](/images/mods/street-rod/diners-starlite.jpg "The Starlite, on the strip")

Up north, the Starlite sits on the neon strip under its sign and a billboard. Three racers were in that night.
