Street Corsa **0.1.6** is out, with **Street Rod 0.5.0**. Get both on the [downloads](/downloads/) page: the game
installs over any earlier version, and installing Street Rod from **Mod Manager → Install mods** brings its places
with it. You need both: the new town needs the new game.

![The Main Street Diner](/images/mods/street-rod/diner.jpg "The Main Street Diner")

**Main Street is a town now.** About five blocks by five: downtown's shops and palms, ranch houses and two-storeys
down the side streets, the neon strip up north, the beach road and the sea to the west, the highway out east and a
farm at the edge, with mountains on the sky beyond. Every save lays it out its own way.

- **The four diners each have a place of their own.** The Main Street Diner stays downtown. The Hi-Way Grill is a
  truck stop out on the highway, with a truck yard and a motel across the road. Surfside Coffee Shop faces the sea on
  the beach road, and the Starlite sits on the strip. Each has its own lot, its own way in and its own bays.
- **Private sellers show their cars all over town.** A car from the paper's private ads stands on a house's drive, at
  a curb, on a vacant lot with a FOR SALE sign, out back of the shops or in a farm yard, the same place every time you
  go and see it.
- **The gas station looks lived in.** Parked cars that change from day to day, the edge of town round it, a back yard,
  a farm up the road, and the attendant in a proper 50s uniform and cap.
- The houses have real hedges and gardens, and the town is drawn near your car and seen far off past it, so it stays
  smooth.

For modders, Street Rod's [street kit](/mods/street-rod-street-kit/) is a mod of its own on SourceForge, shared by
[Main Street](/mods/street-rod-main-street/) and the gas station, and a street can now be a town with every diner at its
own place and its own private sellers' spots. The [modding guide](/tutorials/modding/guide/) has the details.

Everything that changed is in the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
