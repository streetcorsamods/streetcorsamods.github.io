Putting the L79 next to [Doug Broast](https://grabcad.com/doug.broast-1)'s own renders of his
[model](https://grabcad.com/library/l79-chevrolet-small-block-complete-engine-components-1) showed something wrong: our
engine was its mirror image. The starter and the fuel pump were on the driver's side, and the oil filter and the
generator were on the passenger's.

It wasn't just the L79. Every engine pack was drawn the same way, so all of them came out mirrored. They are all fixed
now: every part sits on the same side it does on the real engine.

New versions on the [downloads](/downloads/) page:

- Chevrolet small block 1.5, big block 1.3 and W 1.1
- Pontiac 1.1, Oldsmobile 1.1, Buick 1.1 and Cadillac 1.1
- Ford FE 1.2, Y-block 1.2, Cleveland 1.1 and 385 1.1
- Lincoln-Mercury 1.1

The Ford Falcon six was already right and stays at 2.0. To update, install the new zip from the main screen:
**Mod Manager → Install mods**.

Modders: the fixed versions are tagged in each pack's SourceForge repository.
