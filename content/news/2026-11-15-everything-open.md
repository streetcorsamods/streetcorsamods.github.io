Street Corsa has always been open source. Now the site says it everywhere, and every mod has its files where you can
get at them.

![A Chevy 283 on the stand](/images/mods/street-rod/engine-stand.jpg "A Chevy 283 on the stand, out of a tired '60 Corvette")

## The game has its own page

The game card on [Downloads](/downloads/) now opens [the game's page](/game/), in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
It has the download, the version and its release notes, what you need, pictures, and where everything lives: the code
on [GitHub](https://github.com/streetcorsamods/street-corsa), the mods on
[SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/).

Every mod's page and every Assetto Corsa car's page now ends with the same box: **Open source: make it better**.
It shows the mod's licence, links its repository and the game, and tells you how to join.

## The settings are open too

Until today you could work on the engine packs, Street Rod's places and our Assetto Corsa cars. Now the careers
themselves are open:

- **[Street Rod](/mods/street-rod/)**: [street-rod](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/street-rod/). The
  cars on the lots, the parts and their prices, the dealers, the diners and their regulars, the racers and crews, the
  events, the rules and every line of text.
- **[Touge Nights](/mods/touge-nights/)**: [touge-nights](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/touge-nights/).
  The passes, the racers, the tuning parts.
- **[Chevrolet small block](/mods/chevrolet-small-block/)**:
  [chevy-small-block](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/chevy-small-block/). It joins the other engine
  packs. Thanks again to Doug Broast, whose L79 is the heart of it.

## How to help

Everybody is welcome. You don't need to be a modder to make it better: a bug report, a racer with a better backstory,
a part whose price is wrong for 1970, a translation.

- **The game:** fork it on [GitHub](https://github.com/streetcorsamods/street-corsa), change it, and send a pull
  request. Bugs and ideas go to the [issues](https://github.com/streetcorsamods/street-corsa/issues).
- **A mod:** anyone can download its files. To work on it, open a
  [ticket](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/tickets/) asking to join and you get a branch of your own. What's
  merged goes out in the mod's next version, with your name in its credits. Step by step:
  [Improve our mods](/tutorials/modding/engine-packs/).
- **Your own mod:** a setting, a place, an engine. [Make your first mod](/tutorials/modding/first-mod/).

Players don't need any of this: the downloads stay on the [downloads](/downloads/) page, as always.
