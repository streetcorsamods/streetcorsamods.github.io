Some cars are famous for a race. This one is famous for a bet.

March 1930, Cannes. Woolf Barnato, diamond heir, three-time Le Mans winner and chairman of Bentley Motors, is at
dinner when somebody brings up Le Train Bleu, the luxury express that ran from the Riviera to Calais. Barnato wagers
he can drive to his club in London before the train reaches the coast. He leaves as the train pulls out, drives all
night through rain and fog, crosses the Channel, and is having a drink in St James's four hours before the train
gets to Calais. The French railways were not amused. Bentley fans have been telling the story ever since.

![The Blue Train Bentley in maroon](/images/assetto-corsa/bentley-speed-six-blue-train-1930/shot-front.jpg "Maroon")

The car people picture is the one we built: Speed Six chassis **HM2855**, with the long, low fastback coupé body
Gurney Nutting made for Barnato. The honest version of the story is that its records say it was finished in May
1930, after the run, and the bet itself was won in his Mulliner saloon. But it was the coupé that took the name, and
it's the coupé everybody remembers: the Blue Train Bentley.

![Dove grey: the long bonnet and the low fastback](/images/assetto-corsa/bentley-speed-six-blue-train-1930/shot-side.jpg "Side")

Under that bonnet is the Speed Six engine: 6.6 litres, one overhead camshaft, four valves a cylinder, two SU
carburettors, 180 bhp at 3,500 rpm. A four-speed C-type gearbox with the lever on the right, beam axles on leaf
springs, big drums with a servo, and 21 inch wire wheels. Over two tonnes of car that will still cruise all night.

![Royal blue, the trunk and the 30 BTB plate](/images/assetto-corsa/bentley-speed-six-blue-train-1930/shot-rear.jpg "Rear")

We went through it piece by piece. The dials were redrawn from photographs of the dash, black faces with red-tipped
needles, and they glow at night. It has its own engine sound and a motor Klaxon for a horn. The doors open, the
wipers wipe, the head, pass and tail lamps light up, and a little English mirror hangs from the header on a swan
neck. Underneath, the axles move with the springs. Six skins: the model's black with blue leather, the very dark
green HM2855 is reported to have worn, Bentley racing green, royal blue, maroon and dove grey.
