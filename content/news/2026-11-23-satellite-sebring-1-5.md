Version 1.5 of the [Plymouth Satellite Sebring](/assetto-corsa/plymouth-satellite-sebring-1971/) is out, the second
1971 car to get the full treatment today after the Cutlass Supreme.

The headlights first, because they took a night of driving to get right. A 1971 Satellite has four sealed beams, and
now they do what the real ones do on Custom Shaders Patch: low beam on the outer pair, all four on high beam. Flash
the high beams and all four come on, whether the lights are off or you're cruising on low beam. On the way we found
out why the low beam had been lighting nothing at all: CSP was tying it to the wrong lamps. The turn signals blink
amber in the parking lamps and red in the tail lamps, the rear plate has its lamp, and the gauges stay lit when you
watch the car from outside at night. Thanks to voraciousmyth, who first told us the old headlights were too dim.

The doors open now and the wipers work. The real car hides them under the edge of the hood, so ours park at the base
of the windscreen. Under the car, the rear axle and the differential follow the wheels over the bumps. Hit something
and the paint scratches and the glass and lamps crack; run through the grass and the dust builds up. There are six
onboard cameras, and the driver and the pit crew dress to match each of the sixteen colours.

The far-away versions of the car got a proper going-over too. Seen from a few metres, the old ones showed blue patches
through the vinyl roof, square tyres and a hollow grille. All gone, and the lamps now stay lit on distant cars at night.

Download the new zip from the car's page and drop it onto Content Manager over the old one. And as always, it's open to
anybody who wants to improve it, in its SVN repository on SourceForge.
