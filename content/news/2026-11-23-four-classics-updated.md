After the Cutlass Supreme and the Satellite Sebring, four more of our cars get the same treatment today:
the [Buick Roadmaster 1957](/assetto-corsa/buick-roadmaster-1957/) (1.5), the
[Buick Skylark 1953](/assetto-corsa/buick-skylark-1953/) (1.6), the
[Chevrolet Impala 1960](/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-impala-sedan-1960/) (1.8) and the
[Chevrolet Master DeLuxe 1937](/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-master-deluxe-1937/) (1.5).

All four now have:

- **A rear axle that moves with the wheels.** Watch a replay over a kerb. The Skylark, the Impala and the Master had
  no axle in their models at all, so they got one built for them, with a torque tube or a prop shaft.
- **Kunos-style damage and dirt.** The paint scratches where you hit, the windows and lamps crack, and dust builds up
  when you run wide. A hood or trunk lid comes loose and flaps after a hard hit. Set Damage above 0 % in the assists to
  see it.
- **Wipers that sweep**, with Custom Shaders Patch or in the rain.
- **Better night lights on Custom Shaders Patch.** A real low and high beam, with the warm, slow glow of an old
  filament. Turn signals stay visible even with your foot on the brake, the rear plate has its lamp, and the gauges
  glow when you watch the car from outside. The Impala's four headlamps now work like the real ones: low beam on the
  outer pair, all four on high. Thanks again to voraciousmyth, whose report on the headlights started all this.
- **Six onboard cameras, a light copy of the cabin at a distance, and driver and pit-crew outfits for every skin**,
  as Kunos cars have.
- **Cleaner distant models.** The lamps stay lit on far-away cars at night. The Skylark's red door trim no longer
  shows through its wheel wells, and the Master's lamps stay round at a distance.

The Impala also gets a new driver's eye position, a little higher and further back. The Skylark's wire wheels blur
at speed now.
