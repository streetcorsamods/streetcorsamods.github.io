Street Corsa 0.1.0 is out. Grab it on the [downloads](/downloads/) page.

**It is early access.** The game plays from New Game to the last race, but it is a first version, with work ahead.
Expect rough edges, and saves may not carry over to later versions.

**What's in:**

- The game: the garage, the races in Assetto Corsa, the Mod Manager
- Two settings: **Street Rod** (Los Angeles, 1970) and **Touge Nights** (Gunma, 2002)
- The **Bush DAC10 radio** add-on for the garage

**What's ahead:**

- Engine packs for Street Rod (Chrysler, Chevrolet, Ford, Pontiac and more)
- More places, parts and races
- Balance and polish, from what you find
- A signed installer. Until then Windows may warn that the installer is unknown: **More info → Run anyway**.

**Found a bug?** Tell us on [GitHub](https://github.com/streetcorsamods/street-corsa/issues).
