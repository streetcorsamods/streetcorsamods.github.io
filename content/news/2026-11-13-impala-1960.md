One more for [AC Mods](/assetto-corsa/): the [Chevrolet Impala Sedan 1960](/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-impala-sedan-1960/),
for Assetto Corsa on its own. No Street Corsa needed.

![The 1960 Chevrolet Impala in Roman Red](/images/assetto-corsa/chevrolet-impala-sedan-1960/roman-red.jpg "The 1960 Chevrolet Impala in Roman Red")

It's the four-door sedan with the optional 348 Turbo-Thrust V8: a Carter four-barrel, 250 hp, the two-speed
Powerglide, the X-frame and 7.50-14 whitewalls, with the physics written from the 1960 factory figures and an engine
sound made in engine-sim from the W engine. It has twenty-four skins, the fourteen 1960 Chevrolet colours and the ten
factory two-tones, numbered as Chevrolet numbered them. Each one comes with an Impala houndstooth interior in its own
trim.

Like the other six, it's open to anybody who wants to make it better: its files are in an
[SVN repository on SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/chevrolet-impala-sedan-1960/). Licence:
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Custom Shaders Patch is recommended.
