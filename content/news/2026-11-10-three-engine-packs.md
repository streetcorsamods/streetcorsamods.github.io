Three more engine packs for **Street Rod** are out. Grab them on the [downloads](/downloads/) page.

- **[Chevrolet big block](/mods/chevrolet-big-block/)**: the Mark IV 396, 402, 427 and 454, from the L35 to the L71
  Tri-Power, the aluminium ZL1 and the LS6, and a blown 454 drag engine. The Camaros, Chevelles, Novas, El Caminos,
  Impalas and Corvettes that came with one get it.
- **[Ford FE](/mods/ford-fe/)**: the 352, 390, 406, 427 and 428, from the Galaxie's two-barrel to the Shelby GT500's
  Cobra Jet, and the 427 on its Weber cross-ram, as in the GT40. For the Galaxies, the Fairlane GT, the Thunderbird,
  the Shelbys, the Cobra 427, the Mustang Mach 1 and the F-350.
- **[Ford Y-block](/mods/ford-y-block/)**: the 239 to the Thunderbird 312 and the 1957 E-code dual quad, with a 312
  hot rod on a velocity stack at the speed shops. For the 1956 F-100.

Each one installs like the others: **Mod Manager → Install mods**, pick the zip.

They're on SourceForge too, with the other nine, for anyone who'd like to make them better:
[Improve the engine packs](/tutorials/modding/engine-packs/).
