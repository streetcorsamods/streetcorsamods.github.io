Street Corsa **0.1.5** is out. Get it on the [downloads](/downloads/) page; it installs over any earlier version and
your saves carry over. Street Rod, Touge Nights and the engine packs are unchanged, so you only need the new game.

![Street Rod's garage](/images/mods/street-rod/garage.jpg "Street Rod's garage")

What's new for players:

- **No more busts by a cop you never saw.** A speed-trap cop that Assetto Corsa moved to the pits goes back to its
  trap, and only a cop that really gets past you pulls you over.
- **The garage camera stands in front of the engine hoist**, not behind it.
- **A car in the impound isn't shown in the garage.**
- **Smoother moving scenes**: cars driving in, the car lot, the cruise street and the main screen's fades cost less
  to draw.
- A tow is charged only for the car that was towed, and a crash's cracked windscreen stays cracked.
- A new help page, [People who talk back](/tutorials/playing/ai/), on the AI chat with sellers, salesmen and rivals.

For modders, a setting can now have scored runs alone on public roads in two-way traffic made from your own cars,
Challenges with medals, car tiers, a showroom and high-score tables, a Home front page, a road picker and a 3D
Challenges screen. The [modding guide](/tutorials/modding/guide/) has the details.

Everything that changed is in the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
