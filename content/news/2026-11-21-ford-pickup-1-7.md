The [Ford V8 Pickup 1932](/assetto-corsa/ford-v8-pickup-1932/) was our first car, and version 1.7 is the biggest
update it has had. The goal this time was simple: everything a Kunos car does, this little truck should do too.

It started with a report from Den_88 on OverTake: the rear wheels went up and down, but the axle stayed bolted to the
body. He was right, and now it's fixed properly. The rear axle rides with the wheels, the transverse leaf springs bend
in two halves from the centre clamp, the front beam tilts, and the torque tube and radius rods swing along. Watch a
replay from low down while it goes over a kerb: it's a pleasure.

Then the rest of the list. The doors open, hinged at the front, with the window and handles going along. The vacuum
wiper sweeps the driver's half of the screen. Hit something and the panels get scratched, the lamp lenses and the
windows crack, and the two halves of the hood and the tailgate flap after the knock. Go off the road and the dust
builds up. The wire wheels blur at speed, the tyres get dirty, there are six onboard cameras, and the driver and the
pit crew dress to match each skin.

At night it sees better too: voraciousmyth on OverTake told us our lights were too dim, so the headlights reach further
with Custom Shaders Patch and the stop lamp throws its glow on the road behind. The distant models were rebuilt as
well, so the truck no longer looks shredded at a distance.

Thanks to both of them for taking the time to write. Download the new zip from the car's page and drop it onto Content
Manager over the old one. And as always, it's open to anybody who wants to improve it, in its SVN repository on
SourceForge.
