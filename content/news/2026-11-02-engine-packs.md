title: "Nine engine packs for Street Rod"
date: 2026-11-02T12:00:00
description: Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet W, Ford 385, Cleveland and Falcon six, Lincoln-Mercury, Oldsmobile and Pontiac.
---

The first engine packs are out, for the **Street Rod** setting. Grab them on the [downloads](/downloads/) page.

With a pack installed, the cars that came with its engines get them, part by part: take the engine apart on the stand,
swap the cam, the carbs, the heads, and put it back together.

- **Buick V8**: the Nailhead 364 to 425 and the 400 to 455 big block
- **Cadillac V8**: 331 to 500
- **Chevrolet W**: 348, 409 and the 427 Z11
- **Ford 385**: 429, 460 and the Boss 429
- **Ford Cleveland**: 351C, 351M and 400
- **Ford Falcon six**: the Argentine Falcon's 188 and 221
- **Lincoln-Mercury V8**: the Lincoln Y-block and the MEL
- **Oldsmobile V8**: 400 and 455
- **Pontiac V8**: 389, 400 and 455

Each one installs like a setting: **Mod Manager → Install mods**, pick the zip.

The rest of the packs (Chrysler, the Chevrolet small and big blocks, the Chevrolet six, the flathead, Windsor, FE and
Y-block) are still in the works.
