The **Chevrolet small block** engine pack is on the [downloads](/downloads/) page: eighteen builds, from the 1955 265
to the 1970 LT-1, with the L79 and L76 327s, the Z/28 302, a T56 restomod and the detailed SBC 350.

It's built on [Doug Broast](https://grabcad.com/doug.broast-1)'s [L79 327](https://grabcad.com/library/l79-chevrolet-small-block-complete-engine-components-1), a CAD model of the whole engine down to the valvetrain, that Doug let us use in
the game. Thanks, Doug! Every part comes off on the stand.

[See the pack](/mods/chevrolet-small-block/), or install it from the main screen: **Mod Manager → Install mods**.
