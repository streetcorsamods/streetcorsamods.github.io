This is Street Corsa's new home. News, downloads and tutorials live here.

**What's ready:**

- The game, with its garage, races and Mod Manager
- Two settings: **Street Rod** (Los Angeles, 1970) and **Touge Nights** (Gunma, 2002)

**What's next:** the first release, version 0.1.0. The [downloads](/downloads/) light up when it is out.

The game is free and open source. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/streetcorsamods/street-corsa).
