One more for [AC Mods](/assetto-corsa/): the
[Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme Holiday Sedan 1971](/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-cutlass-supreme-holiday-sedan-1971/),
for Assetto Corsa on its own. No Street Corsa needed.

![The 1971 Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme Holiday Sedan in Matador Red](/images/assetto-corsa/oldsmobile-cutlass-supreme-holiday-sedan-1971/matador-red.jpg "The 1971 Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme Holiday Sedan in Matador Red")

It's the four-door hardtop with the standard Rocket 350 four-barrel: 260 hp, the Turbo Hydra-matic and the 2.56 axle,
with the physics written from Oldsmobile's 1971 dealer specifications book and an engine sound made in engine-sim from
the Oldsmobile V8. The interior is all new, built from photos of real cars, down to the walnut-grain cluster with its
three round gauges. It has sixteen skins, the model's blue and the fifteen 1971 Oldsmobile intermediate colours,
numbered as Oldsmobile numbered them, and each one comes with the interior trim the factory recommended for it.

Like the other seven, it's open to anybody who wants to make it better: its files are in an
[SVN repository on SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/oldsmobile-cutlass-supreme-holiday-sedan-1971/).
