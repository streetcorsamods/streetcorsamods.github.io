The engine packs were made to be built on, and now you can. Their files are on SourceForge, in the
[Street Corsa Mods](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/) project, one repository per pack:

- **Buick V8**, **Cadillac V8**, **Chevrolet W**, **Ford 385**, **Ford Cleveland**, **Ford Falcon six**,
  **Lincoln-Mercury V8**, **Oldsmobile V8** and **Pontiac V8**

Anyone can download them and look inside. If you'd like to make one better (a more detailed block, a missing intake),
ask to join with a [ticket](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/tickets/): you get your own
branch to work on, and what's merged goes out in the pack's next release, with your name in its credits.

How it works, step by step: [Improve the engine packs](/tutorials/modding/engine-packs/).

Players don't need any of this: the packs keep coming from the [downloads](/downloads/) page, as always.
