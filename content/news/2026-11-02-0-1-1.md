Street Corsa **0.1.1** is out. Get it on the [downloads](/downloads/) page; it installs over 0.1.0.

- **Uninstall asks about your saves, settings and mods.** Keep them for the next install, or remove them with the game.
- **Mods installed with the Mod Manager play in your next game**, without restarting Street Corsa.
- **New mods show up in an old save at once.** Shops, junkyards, car lots and the paper restock when you load it.
  Your own cars stay as they are.

The settings and engine packs are the same as before. Everything that changed is in the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
