Mods for **Assetto Corsa itself**, made alongside Street Corsa: you don't need the game to drive them. Classic
American cars, each with its physics written from the factory figures, an engine sound made in engine-sim from its own
engine, working gauges and the colours it came in, named as the maker named them.

**Install:** drag the zip onto [Content Manager](https://acstuff.club/app/), or copy the zip's `content` folder into
your Assetto Corsa folder. [Custom Shaders Patch](https://acstuff.club/patch/) recommended: mirrors, lights, gauges and
the car paint are set up for it.

Each car is open to anybody who wants to improve it: its files are in an SVN repository on
[SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/). Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Looking for Street Corsa's own
settings and engine packs? They're on [Downloads](/downloads/).
