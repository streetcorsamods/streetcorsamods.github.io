Mercedes-Benz SS (W 06), chassis 36348: built in June 1930 as a two-seat works racer, rebodied in 1934 by Erdmann &
Rossi of Berlin-Halensee as a sport roadster for Werner Lüps, and Best of Show at Pebble Beach in 2001. One car, one
body. The M 06 7,065 cc overhead-cam six makes 170 PS; floor the throttle and the Roots blower, turning at three
times engine speed, takes it to 225 PS at 3,300 rpm. Four-speed gearbox (2.75/1.55/1.12/1.00) on a 2.48 axle (2.55
and 2.76 in the setup), beam axle and live axle on semi-elliptic leaves, mechanical drum brakes, 6.50-20 tyres on
wire wheels, 3,400 mm wheelbase, about 1,850 kg, 120 litre tank, 190 km/h. Right-hand drive.

The model told us which car it was: the Erdmann & Rossi coachbuilder's plate and a 1930 plate are painted into its
texture atlas. We built the physics from Mercedes-Benz's own figures for the SS 170/225 and gave it period faces on
the dials: the big rev counter in the middle of the engine-turned dash, the speedometer beside it, both working and
lit at night. Its own engine sound, with the blower's whine rising as the boost comes in.

Like all our cars: rear-hinged doors that open, wipers, a full underside with an axle, leaf springs and a propeller
shaft that move with the suspension, rim blur on the wire wheels, Kunos-style damage and dirt (set Damage above 0 %
in the assists), lamps that light the road, four LODs and a driver and crew dressed for each skin. Custom Shaders
Patch recommended; the car drives without it.

Weight, weight distribution, the centre of gravity and the reverse gear are estimates. The track is the model's
1.67 m, wider than the factory's 1.425 m: the body is drawn around it.
