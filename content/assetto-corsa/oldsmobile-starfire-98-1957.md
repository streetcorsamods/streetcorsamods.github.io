The 1957 Oldsmobile Starfire Ninety-Eight convertible coupe: the top of the Oldsmobile line, 8,278 built at $3,838.
Rocket T-400 V8, 371 cu in, 9.5:1 and a four-barrel: 277 bhp at 4400 rpm and 400 lb-ft at 2800. Jetaway Hydra-Matic
four-speed (3.97/2.55/1.55/1.00) on a 3.42 axle, ball-joint front suspension on coils, a live axle on leaf springs,
power steering and power 11 in drums as standard, 9.00-14 tyres on a 126 in wheelbase, 4747 lb. Every figure in the
physics comes from the 1957 factory specifications.

The cabin was rebuilt to photographs of surviving cars: the one-piece chrome band across the dash with the oval
rocket speedometer set into it, the heater, PNDSLR and air-conditioning strip, the Wonder Bar radio, the clock, the
fluted ivory roll, the glovebox spear and globe emblem, the scalloped steering wheel, and the two-tone pleated
benches and door cards. Underneath, the rear axle, leaf springs, prop shaft and dual exhaust move with the
suspension.

It comes with everything our cars carry now: Kunos-style damage and dirt (set Damage above 0 % in the assists),
cracking glass and lamps, wipers that sweep, headlights with a real low and high beam on Custom Shaders Patch, turn
signals and backup lamps, a lit rear plate, six onboard cameras and a driver and pit crew dressed to match each
colour. Twelve skins in the 1957 Oldsmobile colours, each with its colour-keyed leather. Driven top down: the model
has no raised top.
