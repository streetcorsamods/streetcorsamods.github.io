The 1950 Chevrolet 3100 half-ton pickup, Advance-Design series, with the De Luxe five-window cab. The 216.5 cu in
Thriftmaster valve-in-head six: 6.6:1, 92 bhp at 3400 rpm and 176 lb-ft from 1000 to 2000 rpm. Three-speed column
shift on a 4.11 hypoid axle with torque tube, an I-beam front axle and a live rear axle on semi-elliptic leaves, 11 in
hydraulic drums, 6.00-16 six-ply tyres on 16 x 4.5 steel wheels, 116 in wheelbase, 3175 lb. The physics come from
Chevrolet's own 1950 truck engineering sheets, the torque curve included.

It was built after a restored Swift Red truck: the sun visor, the chrome grille and bumper guards, wide whitewalls with
beauty rings and dog-dish caps, oak stake racks standing in the bed's stake pockets and an oak bed floor, the "Fly like
a bird" hood ornament and the CHEVROLET 3100 hood emblems traced from photographs. The cab was rebuilt to period
photos: the body-colour dash with a working speedometer and combination gauge, the speaker grille, heater, dome lamp
and inside visors, a padded vinyl bench, stamped door panels, doors that open on their concealed hinges, and wipers.

Like all our cars: the rear axle, springs, torque tube and front beam move with the suspension; Kunos-style damage and
dirt (set Damage above 0 % in the assists), glass and lamps that crack, a loose tailgate and bumpers after a hard hit,
six onboard cameras, and a driver and pit crew dressed to match each colour.

The model is Oner Yildirim's, from GrabCAD. We have asked him for permission to share it and will take the mod down
if he objects; please keep crediting him. Our work on it is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
