The 1971 Plymouth Satellite Sebring two-door hardtop, RH23, with the optional 383 cu in V8 four-barrel, the Satellite's
top engine: 300 hp at 4800 rpm and 410 lb-ft at 3400 (SAE gross). The 440 was kept for the GTX and Road Runner.
Three-speed TorqueFlite A727 (2.45/1.45/1.00, without a torque converter), 2.76 axle with 3.23 and 3.55 in the setup,
open differential, torsion bars in front and leaf springs at the rear, 11 in drums, power steering, 14 in wheels, 115
in wheelbase, about 3560 lb. The physics are written from 1971 Satellite figures.

The model was published as a GTX, but it shows a Satellite Sebring, its own scripts and plate included, so that's the
car it is here. Black vinyl roof and buckets, the woodgrain Rallye cluster with working speedometer, fuel and
temperature needles. Sixteen skins: the 1971 Plymouth colours, named as Plymouth named them.
