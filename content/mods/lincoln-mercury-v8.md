Two generations of Lincoln-Mercury V8. The Lincoln Y-block, 317 to 368, from the '52 Cosmopolitan to the Continental Mark II and the '57 Premiere. Then the MEL big block: the Mercury 383 Marauder, the Edsel 410, the Super Marauder 430 on three two-barrels, the Continental's 430 and 462. Plus a Super Marauder crate engine.
