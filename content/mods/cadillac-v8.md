Cadillac's overhead-valve V8 from the 1949 331 that started it, through the 365 and 390 with the Eldorados' dual quads and three two-barrels, to the 429 and the big 472 and 500. Hydra-Matics for the early ones, a Turbo Hydra-Matic 400 for the late ones, and crate engines with headers.
