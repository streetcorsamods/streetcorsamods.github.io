Street Rod's street kit: 86 pieces the places are laid out with. Low shops and brick blocks, ranch houses, two-storeys
and the Fleetwood with their gardens, the cars parked at the curbs and on the lots, tow trucks, palms and street trees,
cobra-head lamps, telephone poles, signals, signs, meters, hydrants, benches and trash cans. Main Street's town and the
gas station both take their pieces from it, so there is one copy of each, picked by its tags. Version 1.1.0 draws every
piece with fewer materials, and the houses have real hedges and shrubs.

## With Street Rod

It's Street Rod's own, and it plays in Street Rod only. It comes inside Street Rod's download: installing Street Rod
installs it too.

## Your own pieces

A mod of your own that plays in Street Rod (`"settings": ["streetrod"]`) adds pieces with a `streetkit` folder: a piece
of a new id with a tag a place asks for (`curb_car`, `house`, `shop`...) turns up wherever that place takes it, and one
of an id here replaces it. The files are on [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/street-rod-street-kit/):
start from them. The [modding guide](/tutorials/modding/guide/) explains the pieces' `piece.json`.

The pieces are made in part from free models from Sketchfab (CC BY 4.0) and Poly Haven (CC0): each piece names its own
in its `piece.json`, and they're all in the credits beside and in the game's Settings.
