The workshop Street Rod is played from: a cinder-block garage with a lift in the middle, and everything standing
round it. The benches and the tool chests, the welder and the compressor, the drill press and the vise, jerrycans,
oil drums, old tyres, a creeper, the engine stand and the hoist. Your car sits on the lift; every place in it is a
button round the car. The garage's air comes with it.

## With Street Rod

It's Street Rod's own garage, and it plays in Street Rod only. Up to Street Rod 0.1.1 it came inside the setting's
download; from Street Rod's next version on it's a mod of its own, and the setting needs it installed.

## Your own garage

The garage was split from Street Rod so it can be swapped. A mod of your own that plays in Street Rod
(`"settings": ["streetrod"]`) and ships a `garages/workshop/` puts another workshop in its place. Its files are on
[SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/street-rod-garage/): start from them. New to modding? The
[modding guide](/tutorials/modding/guide/) starts at the beginning.

The props are free models: most from Poly Haven (CC0), a few from Sketchfab (CC BY 4.0). Each one's credit is in the
list beside, and in the game's Settings.
