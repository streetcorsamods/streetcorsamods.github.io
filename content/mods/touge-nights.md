Gunma, 2002. A tired AE86 in the garage and turbo coupes on the mountain passes after dark: tune your engine, win
touge runs and drift battles, and take the night wind's car.

It races the Kunos Japanese cars, so it **needs Assetto Corsa's Japanese Pack**. It plays in Assetto Corsa's own
showroom until it has a garage of its own, and its engines don't have 3D models yet: the in-game pictures will come
with them.
