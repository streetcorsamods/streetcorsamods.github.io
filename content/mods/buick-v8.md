Two Buick V8s in one pack. The Nailhead, with its small vertical valves, from the 1958 Special's 364 two-barrel to the 425 Super Wildcat on two four-barrels. Then the big block: the GS 400 and its Stage 1, the Wildcat 430, the GS 455 Stage 1 and the Riviera, plus a Stage 1 crate engine with headers.
