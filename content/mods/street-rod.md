The career Street Corsa was made for. Los Angeles, 1970: you start with a little money and a tired old car off a
lot. Take its engine out, build it on the stand part by part, buy from the dealers, the speed shops and the paper, and
race the diners' regulars on Main Street at night for cash and pink slips, until the King of the streets will race you.

Install it first: it's the default setting. The [engine packs](/mods/#engines) give its cars their real engines to
build. New to it? Start with the [Street Rod tutorials](/tutorials/street-rod/).
