The career Street Corsa was made for. Los Angeles, 1970: you start with a little money and a tired old car off a
lot. Pull it apart, build its engine on the stand part by part, buy from the dealers, the speed shops and the paper,
and race the diners' regulars on Main Street for cash and pink slips, until the King of the streets will race you.

## Take it apart

![The car in parts view](/images/mods/street-rod/parts-view.jpg "Parts view: the car turns see-through and every part is yours to take off")

Press **Parts** and the car turns see-through: wheels, brakes, springs, the axle, the gearbox, the engine. Click a
part and take it off, and it goes on your parts shelf. Things come off in order, like on a real car: the radiator
before the engine, the wheels before the brakes, and the game tells you what's in the way. Put the car up on the lift
to get underneath.

## The engine on the stand

![A bare small block on the stand](/images/mods/street-rod/bare-block.jpg "Down to the bare block: heads off, the bores in plain sight")

Pull the engine and it goes onto the stand. Strip it: air cleaner, carb, intake, distributor, valve covers, exhaust
manifolds, heads, down to the bare block. Turn it over to get at the bottom end. Every part you take off lands on the
shelf with its wear on it, and every part you put back changes the power and the torque on the engine sheet.

Then build it your way:

- **Swap parts**: a bigger carb, an aluminium intake, ram's horn manifolds. The speed shops sell them,
  the paper has them cheap, the junkyard has them cheaper.
- **Rebuild them**: worn parts make the car slower. Rebuild a part with a kit, or swap it at the counter.
- **Send it to the machine shop**: bore the block, grind the crank, port the heads. Leave the part, it comes back
  days later.
- **Crate engines and kits** come in a box: assemble them on the stand or straight into the car.

With an [engine pack](/mods/#engines) any block fits any car with an empty engine bay: a big block in a Bel Air, a 283
in a Falcon.

## Buy, sell, haggle

![Ocean Park Autos](/images/mods/street-rod/car-lot.jpg "Ocean Park Autos: the salesman comes out to talk")

Car lots across town, private sellers in the Newspaper, parts dealers and the junkyard. With the
[AI on](/tutorials/playing/ai/), salesmen and sellers talk to you and you haggle in your own words; without it the game
plays the same, only nobody talks back.

## Main Street

![Cruising in town](/images/mods/street-rod/cruising.jpg "Out on the street in a '57 Bel Air")

Press **Go race!** and drive to a diner. Each has its regulars and a horsepower class, and the fast ones won't race
a slow car: pick a racer, a race and a bet. Or cruise the streets, wait at the curb, and somebody pulls up. Drag races,
road races and time attacks, for cash or for pink slips, and the cops come out at night. Work your way up to the King.

## Get it

Install it first: it's the default setting. Its places are mods of their own since 0.4.0, and its download brings them all: the [garage](/mods/street-rod-garage/), the [gas station](/mods/street-rod-gas-station/), the [shops](/mods/street-rod-shops/) and [Main Street](/mods/street-rod-main-street/). The [engine packs](/mods/#engines) give its cars their real engines to
build. New to it? Start with the [Street Rod tutorials](/tutorials/street-rod/).
