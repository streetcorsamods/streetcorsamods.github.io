Chevrolet's Mark IV big block: the 396 from the L35 to the solid-lifter L78, the 402, the 427 from the L36 to the L71 Tri-Power and the all-aluminium ZL1, and the 454 LS5 and LS6. Every build comes with its gearbox, a Muncie M20, M21 or M22 or a Turbo Hydra-Matic, and there's a blown 454 drag engine with a 6-71 and zoomie headers at the speed shops.

Its models are made from Jorma Rysky's "Naturally Aspirated 454 cid V8" and "Blown 454 cid V8" ([Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/Rysky), CC BY 4.0), cut into parts that come apart on the stand, with our own internals, valvetrain, Muncies, Quadrajet and Tri-Power.
