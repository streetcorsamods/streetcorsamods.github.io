Street Rod's Main Street: the street where the racers meet, in front of the diner, and cruise looking for a race. Low
shops along both sides, ranch houses down the cross street, palms, street lights, signals and signs, cars parked at the
curbs. It comes with the street kit it's laid out with: 86 pieces, from the shops and the houses to the parking meters,
and every save lays the street out its own way. The street's air and the diners' come with it.

## With Street Rod

It's Street Rod's own street, and it plays in Street Rod only. Up to Street Rod 0.1.1 it came inside the setting's
download; from Street Rod 0.4.0 on it's a mod of its own, and the setting needs it installed.

The four diners (the Main Street Diner, the Hi-Way Grill, the Surfside Coffee Shop and the Starlite) stay Street Rod's:
who's a regular where, and what's at stake, is the career's. Each one dresses this street its own way, with pieces of
this kit.

## Your own street

A mod of your own that plays in Street Rod (`"settings": ["streetrod"]`) can add pieces to the kit (`streetkit/<piece>/`)
or a street of its own (`streets/<street>/`). The files are on
[SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/street-rod-main-street/): start from them. New to modding? The
[modding guide](/tutorials/modding/guide/) starts at the beginning.

The street is made in part from free models: Quaternius' Downtown City MegaKit (CC0), and the diner, the shops, the
palms, the lights, the signs and the parked cars from Sketchfab (CC BY 4.0). Each one is named in the credits beside,
with what was changed in `CREDITS.txt` in the street's folder, and in the game's Settings. The skies, trees and flowers
are Poly Haven (CC0).
