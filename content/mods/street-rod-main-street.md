Street Rod's town: about five blocks by five round Main Street, where the racers meet at the diners and cruise looking
for a race. Downtown's low shops, palms, street lights and signals; ranch houses and two-storeys down the side streets;
the neon strip up north, the beach road and the sea to the west, the highway out east and a farm at the edge of town,
with the mountains on the sky beyond. Every save lays the town out its own way, and the cars at the curbs change from
day to day. The street's air and the diners' come with it.

- **The four diners each have a place of their own** (2.0.0): the Main Street Diner downtown, the Hi-Way Grill a truck
  stop on the highway with its truck yard and the motel across the road, Surfside Coffee Shop facing the sea, and the
  Starlite on the strip. Each has its own lot, its way in and its bays.
- **The private sellers' spots**: a car from the paper's private ads stands on a house's drive, at a curb, on a vacant
  lot with a FOR SALE sign, out back of the shops or in a farm yard, the same place every time you go and see it.

## With Street Rod

It's Street Rod's own town, and it plays in Street Rod only. It comes inside Street Rod's download: installing Street
Rod installs it too. It's laid out with Street Rod's [street kit](/mods/street-rod-street-kit/), a mod of its own since
Street Rod 0.4.1. Version 2.0.0 needs Street Corsa 0.1.6.

The four diners' regulars, classes and stakes stay Street Rod's: who's a regular where, and what's at stake, is the
career's.

## Your own street

A mod of your own that plays in Street Rod (`"settings": ["streetrod"]`) can add a street of its own (`streets/<street>/`),
a town with its diners at places of their own (`"town": true`) and its own sellers' spots (`"sellers"`). The files are on
[SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/p/streetcorsa-mods/street-rod-main-street/): start from them. New to modding? The
[modding guide](/tutorials/modding/guide/) starts at the beginning.

The town is made in part from free models: Quaternius' Downtown City MegaKit (CC0), and the diners, the motel, the
signs and the trucks from Sketchfab (CC BY 4.0). Each one is named in `CREDITS.txt` in the street's folder, with what was
changed, and in the game's Settings. The skies, with their mountains, are Poly Haven (CC0).
