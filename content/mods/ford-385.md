Ford's 385-series big block: the Thunderbird's 429 Thunder Jet, the Cobra Jet and the Drag Pack Super Cobra Jet, the Continental's 460, a smogged '72 429, and the Boss 429 on its semi-hemi heads. C6 automatics and close-ratio Toploaders, and crate engines with headers for the Super Cobra Jet and the Boss.
