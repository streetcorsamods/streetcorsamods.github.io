Ford's first overhead-valve V8, 1954 to 1964, with its deep skirt and shaft rockers: the 239, the 256 and 272 of the cars and the F-100, the Thunderbird's 292 and 312, and the 1957 E-code with two four-barrels. With the 3-speed, the 3-speed with overdrive and the Ford-O-Matic, and a 312 hot rod at the speed shops: Isky cam, headers and a tall velocity stack on its four-barrel.
