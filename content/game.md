Street Corsa is a free career game. You play it in its own window: buy cars off the lots and out of the paper, take
them apart in your garage, build their engines on the stand and pick your races. The races themselves run in
**Assetto Corsa**, on the cars you have installed, with your parts, your dents and your half-empty tank on them. The
timeslip, the damage and the wear come back to the garage: the engine you raced is the engine you fix.

## A setting is the career you play

The installer is the core: the garage, the races, the rules. The town comes from a **setting**, a mod you install
from the main screen's **Mod Manager**:

- **[Street Rod](/mods/street-rod/)**: Los Angeles, 1970. Muscle cars and Main Street at night, for cash and pink slips.
- **[Touge Nights](/mods/touge-nights/)**: Gunma, 2002. Mountain passes and drift battles.

The [engine packs](/mods/#engines) give the cars real engines to build part by part, and everything else is a mod
too: places, words, rules. [All the mods](/mods/)

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The game installs for your Windows user only, no admin rights.
- **Assetto Corsa** (Steam) with some cars installed.
- **Custom Shaders Patch** 0.2.11 or newer.

[How to install, step by step](/tutorials/playing/install/)

## Early access

It plays from New Game to the last race, but there is work ahead: more engines, places and parts, balance, rough
edges. Saves may not carry over to later versions. What changed in each version: the [changelog](/about/changelog/).
