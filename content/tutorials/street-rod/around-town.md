Everything starts in your garage. Trips take time: far places can't be done in one day.

| Place | What for |
|---|---|
| **Car dealers** | Buy cars. Lots come and go; a closing lot has a sale. |
| **Newspaper** | Cars and parts from private sellers and rivals. Sell your own car here. |
| **Part dealers** | Specialists, speed shops, tyre shops and junkyards. Stock is limited. **Mail order** has everything, takes 3 days. |
| **Machine shop** | On the part dealers' map. Bore, grind, port: leave the part, it comes back days later. |
| **Gas station** | Fuel, oil, a wash. The attendant tells you what's worn. |
| **Paint booth** | New colour. Fix rust and dents first (**Repairs**). |
| **Go race!** | Diners, the street, invitations, Free Run, Test & Tune. |
| **Your cars** | Your warehouse. **TAKE THIS CAR** brings one into the garage. |

## Selling a car

Press **Sell** next to the car's name:

- **Trade it in** at a dealer: cash now, low price.
- **Put it in the paper**: better price, wait for a buyer to call.
- **Scrapyard**: for wrecks.

## Talking (optional)

With the AI on (see [People who talk back](/tutorials/playing/ai.md)) sellers and salesmen talk to you, you can chat with rivals at the
diner and haggle over prices.
Without it the game plays the same, only nobody talks back: the junkyard's **Haggle** and the buyers who answer
your ad still bargain.
